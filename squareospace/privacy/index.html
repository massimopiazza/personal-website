Contact enquiries

If you submit the contact form, Formspree, Inc. receives your name, email address, message, and technical request data such as your IP address and browser information. Formspree processes and stores the submission and forwards it to me by email. Email service providers also process the correspondence to deliver and store messages. If you contact me directly by email, I receive your email address, message, and any other information you choose to send.

I use this information to respond to your enquiry. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR when you request steps towards a contract with me, and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for other correspondence: my legitimate interest in responding to people who contact me. Providing these details is voluntary, but the form requires a name, email address, and message so I can receive and answer your enquiry.

I keep enquiry records while dealing with your request and any necessary follow-up. Once the enquiry is closed and no further correspondence is needed, I delete the records, including copies in my email inbox and Formspree, unless a legal obligation requires retention or the records are necessary to establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim. Records retained for these reasons are kept only for the applicable legal period or while needed for that claim.

More information about Formspree is available in its privacy policy and security information.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures website usage and loading performance. Its browser script sends information such as the page address, referring page, page-load identifier, browser and device characteristics, and loading timings to Cloudflare. This measurement does not use analytics cookies or browser storage to recognise visitors across visits. Cloudflare states that it discards the connection's IP address at its edge and does not store that address in its analytics databases or logs.

Cloudflare retains unsampled measurements for seven days and keeps sampled data for longer-term reporting. Reports for the previous six months are available to the site owner. See Cloudflare's description of the measurements and its analytics retention information.
